Contract terms · contracts/{id} → Terms

A contract records what was agreed; a term records what it costs, with a dated history rather than a single current number — a monthly rent, an annual licence fee, an interest rate. These are the same Term rows, in the same table, under the same domain rules as an account's rate & fee history: the series key is (owner, kind, label), the latest entry on or before a date is the value in force, and supersession is implicit. Only the owner differs — a row hangs off exactly one of AccountId / ContractId. So the section reuses the account surface verbatim: the in-force values as the record's own InfoTiles, then the grouped editable history. What it drops is the hero rate chart — an agreement's price is usually an amount, not a rate.

  • sync_altOne surface, two owners. The in-force InfoTiles, the grouped history, the in-force / superseded / scheduled wording and the cadence phrasing are the account treatment, unchanged. An unlabelled interest rate reads “Interest rate” here and “Interest charged” on a loan, from one helper — not two copies of the wording.
  • paymentsThe currency is a question, not a default. An account lends its own currency to an amount; a contract has none. The field opens unset, the helper says why, and submitting without it is refused inline — the first party's currency and an instance base currency were both rejected as silent assignments.
  • inventory_2Archived is read-only, not hidden. Every write is refused with restoring named as the route that works; the history stays fully readable and the per-row edit / delete controls go away with the writes.
  • production_quantity_limitsThe cap is stated where the writing happens. At ContractMaxTermsPerContract, “New term” is disabled and names the limit (the 422); editing an existing row is exempt, because it replaces a row rather than adding one.
  • shieldThe owner is never in the body. NewTerm carries no owner id, so the route is the only thing that names the contract — a contracts.update holder cannot write a term onto an account, and re-parenting is a delete plus a create.
  • inboxAbsence is not an error. A contract with no terms shows the section's one-line empty state with its create action; an empty currentTerms and a termCount of 0 are healthy responses.
