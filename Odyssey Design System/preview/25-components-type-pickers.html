Contact type pickers

ContactTypeSelect (single) and ContactTypeMultiSelect (filter) are typed, domain pickers pre-wired to the CONTACT_TYPES registry so every option shows its Material icon in its category color, with no per-call option wiring. The single-select delegates to the shared TypeSelect (colored glyph, label, far-right check); the multi wraps MultiSelect. Value is the enum key; everything the control accepts (label, help, error, disabled…) passes straight through.
