Account rate & fee history · accounts/{id} → Terms

A time-versioned history of an account's terms — its interest rate, an optional expected return, and the prices of its bank services (fees) — living as a new section inside the expanded account record, beside Files & Transactions. The latest entry on or before a date is the value in force (implicit supersession — no EffectiveTo). It leads with the interest rate: a step-line chart of the rate over time, a current-terms summary, then the full grouped, editable history. Frontend for the backend AccountTerm spec.

  • show_chartStep line, not a trend. Effective-dated rates hold flat until superseded, so the chart steps — never interpolates — and extends the current value to a dashed Today marker.
  • ruleEligibility is enforced in the dialog. Interest rate only on interest-bearing accounts, expected return only on investment / pension, fees broadly. Ineligible kinds are hidden from the picker.
  • historyHistory is append-style. A newer-dated entry supersedes the prior one in Current terms while both stay visible in the full history; the in-force row is marked.
  • tuneTweak the surface. Toggle the current-terms summary (tiles / row / chips) and the history view (table / timeline) in the bar below.
