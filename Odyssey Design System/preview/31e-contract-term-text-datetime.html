Text & date-time terms · contracts/{id} → Terms

Two new TermValueUnit members let a contract record facts that are not a number: Text (one plain line, up to 256 characters) and DateTime (an instant, stored in UTC). They are ordinary terms: same name field, same effective date, same supersession. They differ in what they leave out. There is no direction, currency, cadence or first-billed date, and no chart point or run-rate contribution. The page below is the full design. The Release switch shows the smaller change the backend issue ships (Goal 9), where the client renders the new kinds correctly but will not edit them.
