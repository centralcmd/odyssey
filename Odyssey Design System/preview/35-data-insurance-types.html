Insurance policy & document types

Two enums power the Insurance Policies feature. The InsurancePolicyType classifies a policy (drives its avatar + the type filter); the PolicyFileType classifies the documents that attach to a policy and to a renewal. Each enum carries only a name — the icon and color are a design-system decision, defined once in the registries so the avatar, the chip and the pickers all render a type identically. Hues share the categorical band with the account / contact registries; the feature's own accent is indigo (oklch hue 282). Other is the default in each and sorts last.
