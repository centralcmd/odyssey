Term cadence & anchor date

A fee's cadence is two fields, not one: Interval names the unit and IntervalCount names how many of them — so every 3 months, every 2 weeks and every 6 months are recordable without the enum growing a value for each. Quarterly is gone (it is Monthly × 3), PerTransaction is the broader PerOccurrence, and PerUnit and Weekly are new. Alongside them, AnchorDate separates when a price took effect from when it is first actually charged — a fee raised on the 1st but billed on the 15th is two dates, and the term could previously record only one.

When the price took effect. Required. Drives sort order, supersession, the in-force projection and the 409 duplicate guard.

First billed on · AnchorDate 2026-01-15

When the term is first actually charged. Optional, fees only. Drives nothing — no schedule, no accrual, no projection, no next-charge date.

  • blockOrdinal 4 is retired, and Weekly does not take it. A stored row still holding the old Quarterly must fail as an undefined value, not silently become something else — so Weekly is 7 and the gap at 4 stays open. The out-of-sequence numbering is the price of that.
  • ruleA count exists only for a periodic unit. The dialog does not disable the field, it does not render it: a "how many" question is never asked about a one-time or per-occurrence charge. Periodic and blank stores 1; non-periodic stores null, never a meaningless 1 that the next read-modify-write would have to defend against.
  • straightenThe bound is one definition. termIntervalCount carries 1–1000 — the same pair the DTO's [Range] and the service's re-check name, so the two layers cannot drift. The dialog validates against the same constant.
  • format_quoteOne wording helper. Chips, tiles, table rows, timeline entries and the dialog's own echo all call cadenceText(interval, count) — monthly · every 3 months · every 2 weeks · per occurrence · per unit. A one-time or unset interval has no cadence and renders nothing at all.
  • labelPerUnit names no unit. The unit is carried by the term's Label — "Custody · per share", "Storage · per box" — not by a field of its own, so the picker stays one enum and the name keeps doing the naming.
  • swap_vertNo ordering between the two dates. Billed in arrears (anchor later) and prepaid (anchor earlier) are both legitimate records, and equal dates are fine — the server refuses neither direction, so neither does the dialog.
  • visibility_offThe anchor is a dialog-only surface. No chip, tile, row or record card carries it: it changes nothing a reader of the list is deciding from, and the in-force term still resolves on EffectiveFrom alone — a term whose anchor is in the future is still current once its effective date has passed. Two entries differing only by anchor still collide with a 409.
