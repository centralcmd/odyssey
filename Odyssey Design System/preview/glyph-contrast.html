Registry glyph contrast

Registry colours stay single authored constants in the L 0.66–0.80 band. Glyphs render them as oklch(from <colour> var(--glyph-l) c h): dark passes l through, light caps it with min(l, 0.58). Soft tints keep the authored colour. Browsers without relative colour syntax drop the declaration and the glyph inherits the text colour.
