When contacts and tags overflow — resolved: T1

The footer wraps, so 4 contacts and 12 tags become three lines — and the attachment count, pinned with margin-left:auto, has no fixed position: it parks at the right end of whichever line the final wrap leaves room on, sharing it with however many chips landed there, and moves as tags are added or removed. On a nearly-full last line it ends up alone. The DS already has a convention for this: TagChips takes a max and rolls the remainder into a +N chip carrying the hidden names in title and in sr-only text — “the detail panel omits max to show them all”. A is both list row and detail, so it follows the detail-panel side of that sentence: no max, every chip shown. Every card above now uses the two-track footer.
