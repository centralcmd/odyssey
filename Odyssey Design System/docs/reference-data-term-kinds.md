# Reference data — Term kinds

> Part of the [Odyssey Design System](../README.md) docs. Foundations, tokens and the component catalog live in the README; this file is the per-feature detail.


The **TermKind** enum classifies each entry in an account's rate & fee history (the **Term** feature — see *Components — Account rate & fee history*). It carries **three** values: two **rates** — **InterestRate** and the optional **ExpectedReturn** — and one **Fee**. As with every Odyssey registry, the enum carries only a name; the **group** (rate / fee), **Material icon**, **color**, and **default unit** are a design-system decision, defined once in `OdysseyData.termKinds` (`ui_kits/web/data.js`) so a kind reads identically in the step chart, the current-terms summary, the history table/timeline, the New term picker, and the account-row subtitle. Specimen: `preview/36-data-term-kinds.html`.

| Kind | Enum | Group | Icon | Color (oklch) | Default unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Interest rate** | 1 | Rate | `percent` | `0.78 0.13 200` (cyan) | Percentage | Contractual interest the account earns or is charged. |
| **Expected return** | 2 | Rate | `trending_up` | `0.72 0.16 295` (violet) | Percentage | Optional target / expected annual return for a variable-return holding. |
| **Fee** | 10 | Fee | `receipt_long` | `0.77 0.14 55` (amber) | Amount | A price the account charges — named by its own label, with its own history. |

**Why three values and not seven.** A kind earns an enum value when the application **treats it differently** — eligibility, ordering, aggregation, or a headline surface that must pick it out; presentation alone is not enough. The two rates qualify: the account row headlines the rate, the step chart picks its series, and the cost-rate tint and "Interest charged" caption key off `InterestRate`, and no label can tell any of those three which number is *the* rate. The four former fee kinds — `ManagementFee` · `ServiceFee` · `TransactionFee` · `OtherFee` — qualified for none of it: all four were eligible on every account type and differed only by a display label, an icon and a default billing period, which is exactly what the term's own `Label` carries better. `Fee` keeps ordinal **10**, the head of the old fee band, so former `ManagementFee` rows need no remap. Two *rate* candidates stay open for a product decision — `EffectiveInterestRate` (*effektiv rente*) and `PenaltyInterestRate` (*forsinkelsesrente*) — because each is a distinct quoted number, not a renaming. Everything else, including every domestic / international and per-currency variant, is a label.

**Value unit (`TermValueUnit`).** `Percentage` — a fraction in `[-1, 1]` (3.40% stored as `0.0340`; negatives allowed); `Amount` — a money value (currency required). Rates are always Percentage; a `Fee` is either, defaulting to Amount.

**Eligibility matrix (`termKindEligibility`).** Which account types a kind can apply to — in code, not the DB, so it changes without a migration. The New term dialog hides ineligible kinds, and where only **one** kind is eligible it renders no picker at all.

| Kind | Eligible account types |
|---|---|
| `InterestRate` | Checking · Savings · Pension · Credit card · Mortgage · Student loan · Personal loan · Car loan · Tax debt |
| `ExpectedReturn` | Investment · Pension |
| `Fee` | All account types |

**A term has two possible owners.** The same enum classifies a term owned by a **contract** (see *Components — Contract terms*), where eligibility is not a matrix: **`Fee` and `InterestRate` on every `ContractType`**, `ExpectedReturn` refused — it prices invested principal, which a contract does not hold. The contract picker therefore always renders both kinds. The one other owner-dependent rule is currency: an `Amount` term defaults to the **account's** currency, and on a contract it must be given explicitly, because a contract has none to lend.

**Series labels (`Label`).** A term also carries a user-authored **`Label`** (≤ 64 chars), and the series key is **`(owner, TermKind, LabelKey)`** — where *owner* is the populated one of `AccountId` / `ContractId`, so an account term and a contract term with the same kind and label never interact — so one kind can hold several concurrently in-force terms: a card prices *ATM withdrawal · domestic* and *ATM withdrawal · abroad* separately, each keeping its own price history. The label is **refused on the rate kinds** (`InterestRate` / `ExpectedReturn` — the headline rate must stay unambiguous, and a labelled second rate would have no non-arbitrary tiebreak) and **required on every `Fee`**: with one fee kind, two unnamed fees cannot be told apart, so the old `OtherFee`-only rule collapses into one unconditional rule. Labels are normalized by one shared rule — trim, collapse internal whitespace, blank → `null` — and matched case-folded (`LabelKey`), so *"ATM abroad"*, *"atm abroad"* and *"  ATM   abroad  "* are the same series. A rate is therefore always the **unnamed series** of its own kind, exactly how it resolved before labels existed.

**Cadence (`Interval` + `IntervalCount`).** Optional context for fees, null for rates, and **two fields**, not one. The **unit** is `Interval` — **OneTime(0) · PerOccurrence(1) · Daily(2) · Monthly(3) · PerUnit(6) · Weekly(7)**, plus `Annually(5)`; each carries a glyph + categorical hue in `OdysseyData.intervals` (`looks_one` gold · `bolt` magenta · `straighten` amber · `today` cyan · `view_week` teal · `calendar_month` blue · `event_repeat` violet), so the Billing-interval picker reads like the Subscriptions one. The **multiplier** is `IntervalCount` (1–1000), so "every 3 months" and "every 2 weeks" are recordable without an enum value each. `Quarterly` is **gone** — it is Monthly × 3 — and its ordinal **4 is retired, never reassigned**, so a stale row holding it must fail as undefined rather than silently become `Weekly`. `PerTransaction` is renamed **`PerOccurrence`** (same ordinal 1); `PerUnit` names a charge per unit of something, and **the unit itself is carried by the term's `Label`** ("Custody · per share"), not by a field of its own.

A count exists **only for a periodic unit** (`Daily` / `Weekly` / `Monthly` / `Annually`): periodic and blank stores **1**, non-periodic stores **null** — never a meaningless 1. Every surface words the pair through one helper, `OdysseyHelpers.cadenceText(interval, count)`: *monthly* · *every 3 months* · *every 2 weeks* · *per occurrence* · *per unit*; a one-time or unset interval has **no cadence** and renders nothing. A new fee defaults to **Monthly** — one default, since there is no longer a fee kind to guess from.

**Anchor date (`AnchorDate`).** Optional on fees, refused on rates: **when the term is first actually charged**, as distinct from when its price took effect. A fee raised on the 1st but not billed until the 15th is two dates, and the term could previously record only one. The server imposes **no ordering** against `EffectiveFrom` — arrears (anchor later) and prepaid (anchor earlier) are both legitimate — and **nothing is derived from it**: it does not schedule, accrue or project, it never enters the series key, supersession or the `409` duplicate guard, and `GET …/terms/current` still resolves the in-force term on `EffectiveFrom` alone. It is authored and read in the **New / Edit term dialog only** ("First billed on"); no list, tile or chip carries it.

> **Encoding rule.** Like `accountTypes` / `contactTypes` / file-type registries, these hues identify a *kind* and share the categorical chroma/lightness band (L ~0.74–0.80, C ~0.13–0.16) so all the registries read as one family. They never encode income / expense / status, and brand **tide** stays out of the scale. Keep the registry keys in lockstep with the C# `TermKind` enum values.
