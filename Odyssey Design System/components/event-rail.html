Nodes are neutral by default — the glyph names the kind, and holding the hue back keeps a column of colours from competing with status. Row actions sit inline after the date; the provenance line (.odc-er-meta) reveals on the same hover — on an item, and on a marker given meta, as the closing endpoint here is.

The provenance line is where a row says where it came from — who recorded it, and, on a row the application recorded itself, that it was automatically generated. It lives in .odc-er-meta, so it is revealed by the same hover as the row actions: nothing about origin is visible at rest.
