ContactChip is the canonical read display of a linked or tagged contact. It composes the chip visual language (.odc-chip) with the canonical ContactType registry — the type glyph and its category color come from CONTACT_TYPES, never re-hardcoded. Crucially, People tagged on a photo or record are Contacts of type Person, not app Users — so they render with this chip (person glyph in the Person color), keeping one consistent people/contact language across the Journal, Photos, and transaction surfaces.
