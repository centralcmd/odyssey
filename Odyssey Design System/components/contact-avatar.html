Fit is chosen from what the image IS, not from the shape it lands in. A person's photograph has a subject in the middle and survives a square crop, so it is cover in a circle. A logo is a shape on transparency with no safe crop — often a wide wordmark — so it is contain on a neutral ground. The two contact types share one storage slot and one upload pipeline; only the framing differs.

The mark is decorative in a list. alt="" on the record mark: the adjacent cell already names the contact, and an alt there would read the name twice. Where the image is the subject of its own Change / Remove controls — the identity tile — it gets a descriptive alt built from the resolved display name, never the nullable one. Every list image also carries loading="lazy" and decoding="async": a 50-row view must not decode fifty pictures to show ten.

The accent does not move with the image. accent / accentSoft still set --rec / --rec-soft for every icon chip and single-series chart in the card. What the image mark changes is the mark's content, and for a contained logo the mark's own ground.
