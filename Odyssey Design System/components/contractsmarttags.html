One component, two hosts. On a contract the watchlist answers what this agreement actually cost, beside the Terms section that says what it is contracted to cost — the tags resolve through the transactions endpoint, so the match is by tag and carries no contract scope. The cap is ContractMaxSmartTagsPerContract (default 20, ceiling 50 — the filter-array length the resolution query accepts); it is served by the claim-free /api/contract-limits and interpolated, never held here as a constant. At the cap the adder blocks and the bar says the number; a refused write shows the server's own sentence; a degraded (503) limits read leaves the number unknown, so the client stops pre-checking and the server's conservative bound does the refusing.
