Subscriptions <BillingIntervalSelect /> · <BillingIntervalChip /> · <SubscriptionStatusChip />

The Subscriptions feature vocabulary. BillingIntervalSelect / BillingIntervalMultiSelect are typed wrappers over the shared TypeSelect / MultiSelect, fed the BILLING_INTERVALS registry (Daily · Weekly · Monthly · Yearly, in enum order). BillingIntervalChip shows a subscription's cadence with the per-cycle billing anchor derived from firstBillingDate + interval at render time — never stored, and honouring the count ("every N") multiplier. SubscriptionStatusChip renders the two independent lifecycle flags (Paused · Archived) as text-labelled chips; the meaning never rides on colour alone.
